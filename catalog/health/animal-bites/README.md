## Processing Notes

Mirrored from the City of St. Louis open data portal (https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/data/datasets/dataset.cfm?id=130). Nothing was added to the data and no features were dropped except where noted below.

Downloaded from the city's portal: https://static.stlouis-mo.gov/open-data/HEALTH/ANIMAL-CONTROL/ANIMAL_BITES.csv (CSV).

The city geocodes these to addresses; a handful land well outside the city, so points were clipped to the city boundary with a small buffer.

Converted to GeoParquet with gpio — zstd compression, Hilbert row order, and a covering bbox column with row-group statistics, so a spatial filter can skip most of the file over the network — and tiled to PMTiles with tippecanoe.

The city's own file(s) are published as `source` assets on this collection, linked directly to stlouis-mo.gov — this mirror never becomes the only way to reach the original.
